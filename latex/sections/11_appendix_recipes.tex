\section{Crafting Recipes}
\label{app:recipes}

Table~\ref{tab:recipes} lists the crafting recipe for every item provided by the mod. All
twenty use only vanilla Minecraft ingredients, with no dependency on any other mod. Every
recipe listed is shapeless (order-independent) except the resistor, capacitor, inductor,
power supply, function generator, breadboard, probe, X-Y probe, AC probe, and the R2V/V2R
converters, which require their ingredients arranged in the specific pattern noted in the
table -- a horizontal $1\times3$ ``lead--body--lead'' row for the resistor/capacitor/inductor,
a vertical $1\times3$ column for the three probes, and a full $3\times3$ grid for the rest. The
resistor and inductor each accept either of two interchangeable materials for their body
ingredient, noted as ``X or Y'' in the table.
